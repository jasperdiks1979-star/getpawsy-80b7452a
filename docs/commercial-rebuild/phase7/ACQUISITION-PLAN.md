## UTM taxonomy

`utm_source` = `google` \| `pinterest` \| `tiktok` \| `email` \| `partner`
`utm_medium` = `cpc` \| `social` \| `organic_social` \| `email` \| `referral`
`utm_campaign` = `{hero_id}-{objective}-{yyyymm}` e.g. `h1-litter-tracking-202610`
`utm_content` = creative variant id, e.g. `vid-a`, `static-b`
`utm_term` = keyword for search only

The existing analytics already classifies channels from these parameters; no new tracking code is required.

## Creative brief template

- **Problem** — one sentence, stated from the cat owner's side
- **Proof** — only verified product facts (material, capacity, origin). No ratings, no customer counts, no testing claims, no delivery promises
- **Offer** — the real one: free shipping over $35, up to 20% off when buying 2+ items (both honoured at checkout)
- **CTA** — direct to the hero PDP, never to the homepage
- **Do not use** — star ratings, "bestseller", "#1", "trusted by N owners", "vet tested", any specific delivery day

## Gate before any spend

1. At least 10 paid orders so conversion rate is measurable
2. Approved delivery-time evidence from the supplier
3. Explicit owner approval per channel and budget

Until all three are met, the recommendation is organic only: product pages, category pages and the cat-first content clusters already live.
